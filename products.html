Buyer's Guide

How to choose: Electric Scooter vs. Electric Bike?

Both give you clean electric mobility, but their ergonomics and daily convenience differ depending on your routine.

🛴 Choose an E-Scooter if:

You live in an apartment without bike parking, commute by metro part-way, or want a compact ride you can easily carry upstairs or keep under your office desk.

🚲 Choose an E-Bike if:

Your commute is over 15 km each way, you carry groceries regularly, you want mild aerobic exercise with pedal assist, or frequently tackle bumpy uneven roads.
