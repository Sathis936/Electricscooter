Showroom FAQ

Visiting Our Showroom & Service Bay

Everything you need to know before visiting in person.

Do I need an appointment to visit the showroom?
Walk-ins are warmly welcome six days a week during our normal hours. However, if you plan to do a comprehensive 30-minute test ride, booking in advance ensures your preferred model is 100% charged and adjusted to your height before you arrive.
How do I get there by public transit or car?
We are conveniently located two blocks south of the Riverside Metro Central Station (Green Line). If driving, we provide dedicated customer parking bays with complimentary Level-2 EV charging right in front of our showroom entrance.
Can I bring an existing electric scooter or bike for repairs?
Yes! Our workshop desk services EcoRide models as well as most major third-party electric micro-mobility brands. We perform inner tube changes, brake bleeds, rotor truing, battery diagnostic load tests, and controller replacements.
