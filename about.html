Proprietary Engineering

Next-Generation EV Powertrain & Battery Tech

What sets an EcoRide scooter apart is what lies underneath the deck.

Automotive-Grade Lithium Cells

We utilize Tier-1 LG and Samsung 21700 NMC cells that deliver up to 25% higher energy density than standard 18650 cells, retaining over 80% capacity even after 1,200 charge cycles.

Smart BMS 2.0 Protection

Our intelligent Battery Management System constantly monitors cell temperatures, overcurrent, and short circuits with automatic fail-safes and micro-second cutoff.

KERS Kinetic Energy Recovery

When you squeeze the brake levers, the motor converts kinetic deceleration energy back into electric current, extending your range by up to 15% in stop-and-go downtown traffic.

Quality Assurance

Tested for Extreme Road Realities

Every frame and battery pack is put through brutal real-world torture tests before entering production.

100,000 Rigorous Pothole Drop Durability Tests

Frames undergo automated pneumatic drop-impact testing simulating 100,000 severe pothole impacts at full 130 kg payload capacity.

Nail Penetration & Thermal Runaway

Battery enclosures feature multi-stage fire-retardant thermal barriers and pressure relief valves that prevent thermal propagation even under puncture.

Continuous Submersion Waterproofing

Electronics are sealed in IP67 silicon resin potting, tested under 1 meter of standing water to guarantee zero moisture ingress during severe monsoons.

Full After-Sales Service

Local workshop support, not distant overseas call centers.

Most internet scooter sellers disappear the minute a brake cable slips or a tire punctures. EcoRide Hub operates a dedicated brick-and-mortar service workshop with factory-trained technicians right inside our showroom.

🔧 6-Point Pre-Delivery Inspection (PDI)

Every vehicle undergoes torque verification, brake rotor alignment, tire pressure calibration, and firmware flashing before you take delivery.
