100% Road Tax Exemption

Electric two-wheelers qualify for zero road tax and registration fee waivers in most states, saving ₹6,000–₹10,000 upfront compared to petrol scooters.

Clear Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about battery charging, legal road use, and ownership.

How is real-world battery range determined?
We test all vehicles under realistic conditions: a 75 kg rider, ambient temperature of 28°C, riding on standard city asphalt at 20–25 km/h with 10 stoplights. Unlike lab treadmill figures, our published range matches what you will actually experience on city roads.
Can I ride safely in heavy monsoon rain and waterlogged streets?
Yes. Our flagship models like the Comet Urban Pro and Volt Aero X carry IP67 water ingress certification. All hub motors, controller harnesses, battery compartments, and dashboard displays are sealed against downpours and shallow road splashing.
How much does a full recharge cost on my electricity bill?
A typical 500Wh battery takes about 0.5 units (kWh) to charge completely. At typical domestic electricity rates (₹6 to ₹8 per unit), a full recharge costs between ₹4 and ₹8. That is under ₹200 per month for daily commuting!
Do I need a driver's license or RTO registration?
Vehicles limited to a maximum speed of 25 km/h with motor output up to 250W (like the Swift Fold S2 and City Lite Go) are classified as non-motorized micro-mobility and do not require RTO registration or a driver's license. High-speed models (over 25 km/h) require a standard driving license.
